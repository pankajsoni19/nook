# Card estimates (lane ESTIMATES)

Operator, 2026-11-13: estimates on task cards, with a board-level setting that chooses what they mean (off, story points, or hours), editable in Board settings; a field on the card, a badge on card faces, column and sprint totals, and the same over REST v1 and MCP.

Decisions **D750–D757** (D710–D749 are the parallel lanes' of the same day). Migration **082** (079 is board keys, 080 start dates, 081 reserved for card activity). No new threat: estimates are one bounded number per card, written at the card's edit level and the board's manage level like every other field.

This **supersedes Q4** (and the last sentence of **D134**, "No story points in v1") of [the hierarchy research](research/2026-09-26-task-hierarchy-workflows.md) ("Story points and a burndown chart? No, not in v1 … Add an optional `estimate` field later if a scrum team asks") and the "no points (Q4)" notes in `SprintBar.tsx` and `sprintModel.ts`. A burndown chart is still out of scope.

## Common practice (short survey, to choose the scales)

- **Jira**: "Story points" is a plain number field (any value, decimals allowed); teams that use planning poker pick Fibonacci-like values (0, ½, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 20, 40, 100). Time tracking is a separate "Original estimate" in hours/days. Boards sum story points per column and per sprint, and sprint reports show completed vs. remaining points.
- **Linear**: each team turns estimates on and picks a scale: exponential (1, 2, 4, 8, 16), Fibonacci (1, 2, 3, 5, 8), linear (1–5), or T-shirt sizes; an "extended" option adds larger values and 0. Cycles and projects show estimate totals beside counts.
- Taken from both: one setting per board (Linear's per-team switch), a Fibonacci scale as the points default (the most common planning-poker scale, starting at 0 for "trivial" and capped at 21 since larger stories are usually split), a free numeric option for teams who use Jira-style numbers, and hours as a separate kind rather than a second field. T-shirt sizes and exponential scales are left out to keep the design small (they map onto points later without a schema change: the scale is a CHECK-listed text column).

## Decisions

| ID | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D750 | **A board chooses one estimate kind**: `boards.estimate_kind` is `none` (the default for every board, new and existing: no field, no badge, no totals), `points`, or `hours`. A card stores one number, `cards.estimate` (REAL, NULL for none), whose meaning comes from its board. Changed by the board's owner or a manager (`PATCH /boards/:b { estimateKind?, estimateScale? }`, the same level as auto-archive and structure), in **Board settings → Estimates** (`EstimateSettingsSection.tsx`), saved on its own. Audited `task.board_estimates { boardId, kind, scale, kindBefore, scaleBefore, estimatedCards }`; a save that changes nothing writes no row. MCP reads the settings but cannot change them (structure stays the owner's through a key, D265). | A per-board switch keeps boards that do not estimate free of the field (Q4's concern). One column for the kind instead of a field per kind keeps the schema and the API small. |
| D751 | **Points have a scale**: `boards.estimate_scale` is `fibonacci` (0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21; the default) or `free` (any number in range). It is kept, but unused, for hours and none, so turning points back on restores the team's scale. | Fibonacci is the common planning-poker scale and gives a chip picker instead of typing; free covers Jira-style numbers (½, 4, 40). |
| D752 | **Changing a board's kind or scale never rewrites stored values.** Off **hides** them: every read (`GET /boards/:b`, `GET /cards/:k`, card writes, `POST /query`, MCP) returns `estimate: null` while the board's kind is `none`, and `has:estimate` matches nothing there; turning estimates on again shows them. **Points ↔ hours keeps the numbers** (5 points read as 5 hours); the settings section says so before saving ("Values are not converted …"). Switching to the Fibonacci scale keeps values off the scale; the card field shows "Now 4 points, from before the board used the Fibonacci scale" until someone picks a value, and other edits to that card are never refused because of it (only an `estimate` write is checked). After a kind change the board reloads, so the kept values appear. | Nothing is lost by trying a setting; a conversion rule (1 point = N hours) would be a guess. Hiding at the read keeps every client, including MCP agents, from showing numbers the board says are off. |
| D753 | **Validation** (`shared/estimates.ts` `checkEstimate`, the same for REST and MCP): `estimate` is a JSON number or `null` on `POST /boards/:b/cards` and `PATCH /cards/:k` (anything else is the schema's 400). `null` always clears, even with estimates off. Otherwise 400 `ESTIMATES_OFF` ("Turn estimates on in Board settings first") while the board's kind is `none`, and 400 `ESTIMATE_INVALID` for a value outside **0–999**, with **more than two decimals**, or, on a Fibonacci board, **not on the scale** (the message lists the scale). The database also CHECKs 0–999. A patch that sets only `estimate` is a card edit: `revision + 1`, the CARD_CHANGED compare-and-swap, audited in `task.card_update { estimate }` (and `task.card_create { estimate }`). MCP maps both codes to `INVALID` with `reason`. | 999 hours is half a working year and 999 points is beyond any real story; two decimals allow quarter hours. Coded errors in the existing style (`SPRINTS_OFF`, `LEVEL_INVALID`) let clients explain them. |
| D754 | **In the app**: the card dialog and page show an **Estimate** field below the card's fields (`EstimateField.tsx`): on a Fibonacci board a row of chips that save on a click (clicking the chosen one clears it); on a free points or hours board a number input with its unit ("pts" or "h") that saves on Enter or blur, empty clearing it, with the server's rule checked first; read-only roles see text. A compact chip ("5 pts", "1 pt", "3h", "1.5h", read as "estimate 5 points") on card faces (board, swimlanes), in the list view's rows, and as a sortable **Estimate** column in the table (`sort=estimate`, empty last both ways). Column headers show the total of the cards they list ("13 pts", under the board's filters, sprint, and levels), only when one of them has an estimate. With estimates off none of this renders. The composer does not offer an estimate yet (set it in the card once created). | The same chip vocabulary as the due chip and counts; the totals follow what is on screen, as the column count does. |
| D755 | **Sprint totals**: the sprint bar adds "13 of 21 pts done · 8 pts left" beside "8 of 14 done" (the backlog: "21 pts"), summing the same work-level cards the counts use, by their column's state; a subtask's estimate never adds to its parent's sprint (no double counting). Hidden with estimates off or when no card in it has one. MCP: `list_cards` returns `board.estimates: { kind, scale }` and each card's `estimate`; `get_card` adds `estimate_kind`; `create_card` and `update_card` take `estimate`. REST v1 runs the same tools. `list_sprints` is unchanged (counts only). | Points done vs. remaining is what a scrum team reads from the strip; no burndown chart (still out of scope). |
| D756 | **Query**: `has:estimate` (and `-has:estimate`) in the task grammar, on the server compiler and the board's in-memory matcher alike; `POST /api/tasks/query` results carry `estimate` and `estimate_kind` per card. There is no numeric range filter (`estimate:>3`): no other field has one, and it would need a grammar for comparisons. | A cheap way to find unestimated cards before planning, consistent with `has:subtasks`. |
| D757 | **Out of scope**: converting values between kinds, T-shirt sizes or other scales, estimates in the composer and in Tasks home lanes' faces (their results carry the values for a later pass), roll-ups of subtask estimates onto parents, and a burndown chart. | Keep the lane small; each can come later without a schema change. |
